About the game

Battle Royal IRL drops you into a huge, freshly generated battlefield every time you load the page: 2 km × 2 km of hills, border mountains, nine towns, roads, around 200 enterable houses and warehouses, 5,000 trees and wrecked cars.

You fight for Blue. Everyone spawns with nothing but their fists, so the first minutes are a race to loot pistols, SMGs, rifles, snipers and, if you're lucky, the rocket launcher. Then the zone starts closing in, and the two teams are forced together until one side is wiped out.

Pick each team's size from 1 to 50 players and set the bot difficulty separately, from Easy all the way to Impossible. Bots loot, roam, react to gunfire, throw grenades and heal, exactly like you. Die, and a killcam shows who got you, then you can spectate your team in first person, third person or free look.

Features

Built for real fights

⚔

Up to 100 players

Set each team from 1 to 50 players with its own bot difficulty. Both sides get the same loot at the same distances, so every round is fair.

✥

14 weapons in 3 tiers

From the Glock and Desert Eagle to the AK-47, M4A4 and P90, up to the AWP, M249 and a rocket launcher. Headshots always do double damage.

⚙

Detailed gunplay

Stance-based recoil, aim down sights, and full reload animations: magazines drop out, bolts are pulled and shotgun shells go in one at a time.

🚗

Drive anything

About 100 cars are parked along the roads. Get in, cross the map, and run down enemies. The car body stops bullets, but the windows don't.

💣

Grenades & medkits

Frag grenades bounce and blast, smoke blinds the bots, and medkits heal half your health bar. Bots use all of them too.

🎥

Killcam & spectating

See exactly who got you and how, then follow your team in first person, third person or a free-flying camera.
