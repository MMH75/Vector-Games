About the game

Start in the garage, spin your car around on the turntable and choose from six very different machines: the Vortex GT sports coupe, the Apex R1 hypercar, the Phantom V8 muscle car, the tiny Bolt Mini, and two 4x4s, the Titan X4 SUV and the Ridgeback pickup.

Then hit the road. The City is a sprawling downtown of skyscrapers, diagonal avenues and landmarks like the Spire Tower, the Sky Needle, a stadium and a Ferris wheel. The Hills are over a kilometre of rolling countryside with a ring road, Crystal Lake, farms, wind turbines and a lookout point.

Every junction has working traffic lights, and AI drivers keep to their lane, signal, give way and stop at red. Crash into someone in front of a cop, though, and the sirens come on. Lose them in the streets, or get busted.

Features

A world that drives back

🏎

Six unique cars

Sports cars, a hypercar, muscle, a city hatchback and two 4x4s, each with its own speed, handling, toughness and cockpit dashboard.

🏙

Two open maps

A dense downtown full of landmarks, or rolling hills where grass slows you down and 4x4s shine off-road.

🚦

Living traffic

Up to 108 AI cars that follow lanes, signal at junctions, obey traffic lights, and spin realistically when you hit them.

🚨

Police chases

Cause a crash in front of a cop and you're wanted. Police route through the streets to hunt you down. Stay out of sight to escape.

🌙

Day & night

At night: a starry sky, lit office windows, street lamps, glowing towers and real headlight beams on your car.

🎥

Four cameras

Chase, far chase, hood and a full cockpit view, plus working indicators, hazards, horn and headlights.
