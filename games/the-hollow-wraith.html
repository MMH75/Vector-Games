About the game

You wake up in the foyer of an abandoned manor, half rotting old house and half decaying hotel. The front door is locked, and you are not alone.

Somewhere in the dark, the Hollow Wraith drifts through the corridors: a hooded, skull-masked creature with long hooked claws and a serpent's tail, hovering above the floorboards. It wanders, it listens, and the moment it sees you, it runs.

To escape, search the house for the keys to the front door. They're hidden in dusty bedrooms, a crumbling library, a nursery and dozens of numbered hotel rooms, some lying on the floor and some tucked away in drawers. Find four of them, fit each one into the locks, and get out.

Inside the manor

Features

Something is listening

🏚

Two floors to explore

A ground-floor manor of bedrooms, a library, a kitchen and a grand hall, and an upstairs hotel with over fifty numbered rooms.

👁

A hunter that remembers

The Wraith only sees what's in front of it. It loses you around corners, runs to where it last saw you, and searches before giving up.

🚪

Hide or run

Sprint on limited stamina, or slip into a louvred cupboard and watch it pass by through the slats.

🔦

Atmosphere first

Flickering lamps, a failing flashlight, lightning through the windows, creaking doors and a presence you hear before you see.

☠

Your kind of nightmare

Choose 1 to 10 Wraiths and Easy, Medium or Hard, which changes how fast they move and how many keys are hidden.

⚡

No installation

Everything, from the textures to the house itself, is generated in code. Open the link and step inside.
